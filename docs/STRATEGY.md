# Strategy

The long side selects the contract whose completed daily close is nearest its maximum recorded MEXC perpetual daily high. The short side selects the contract whose completed daily close is nearest its minimum recorded MEXC perpetual daily low. Both ranks are percentage-based and sort ascending. This is the selected Option A interpretation, not a contrarian farthest-from-extremes rule.

The system makes no assumption that these extremes predict future returns. The hypothesis requires independent forward evaluation net of fees, funding and realistic simulated execution friction.
